# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

## Project Overview
An end-to-end e-commerce analytics project focused on analyzing sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, regional performance, monthly trends, and discount impact.

The project combines Excel, Python, SQL, and Power BI to transform raw transaction data into business insights and an interactive dashboard.

## Business Objective
- Evaluate overall sales and profitability.
- Identify high-performing products and categories.
- Analyze regional and customer performance.
- Understand monthly sales trends.
- Measure the relationship between discounts and profit margin.
- Present actionable insights through Power BI.

## Key Business Insights
- Total Sales: **₹210.46M**
- Total Profit: **₹25.56M**
- Overall Profit Margin: **12.15%**
- Total Transactions: **8,000**
- Unique Customers: **1,199**
- Unique Products: **30**
- Categories: **5**
- Regions: **5**
- Electronics generated the highest category sales at approximately **₹120.88M**.
- Smartwatch was the top product by both sales and profit.
- South was the highest-sales region at approximately **₹65.88M**.
- Profit margin declined as discount levels increased, indicating that excessive discounting can reduce profitability.

## Business Recommendations
- Focus on high-performing products while monitoring their profitability.
- Review regional performance to identify growth opportunities.
- Use customer-level analysis to identify and retain high-value customers.
- Optimize discount strategies to protect profit margins.
- Monitor monthly sales trends for better planning and forecasting.
